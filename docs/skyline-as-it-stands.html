What the app does today · 29 Aug 2026

SkyLine as it stands

Not what it is meant to be — what it actually does right now, on a real phone. 38 screens, 44 user stories. Twenty-three work, eight have caveats, thirteen are broken.

What this app is today: it reads your photo library, works out the places you spent time in, and deals them to you one card at a time to judge Worth it, Fine or Skip. What you judge becomes a searchable log, grouped by country and year, sitting over a globe.

Wrapped around that is a second, older app — a flight tracker with trips, boarding passes and itineraries. It is roughly half the code and it no longer serves the first thing.

The path a new user actually walks

Twelve steps from launch to a filled log. Five of them are broken, marked in red.

  1. 1
    Splash

    Checks for a stored credential.

  2. 2
    Sign in

    Usually skipped — iOS silently re-authenticates a cached Apple credential, so most launches never show this. Google is non-functional.

  3. 3
    Wait for the globe

    The sheet holding the entire app cannot appear until a WebGL globe loads from a CDN. Three-second gate.

  4. 4
    Onboarding, page 1 — the premise

    “Everywhere you went, and whether it was worth it.” Three verdicts shown as peers.

  5. 5
    Page 2 — practise a verdict

    A real card and three real chips. Will not advance until you tap one.

  6. 6
    Page 3 — photo primer

    Explains what it will read, then the system permission prompt.

  7. 7
    The scan

    Clusters the whole library by GPS and time into episodes. On this phone: 25,936 photos, ~120 places across 21 trips.

  8. 8
    Scan summary

    “Review 6 places in Shibuya”, with the rest kept for later.

  9. 9
    The deck

    One place per card. Worth it / Fine / Skip, one tap. This part is good.

  10. 10
    Deck summary — never seen

    The one screen that states the thesis in words is destroyed on the frame it appears, because onboarding advances on deck finished instead of on its Done button.

  11. 11
    The place log

    Everything files under one “Unknown” country, padded with airports from four demo flights you never took.

  12. 12
    No way back

    The scan cannot be run again. There is no re-entry point anywhere in the app.

User stories

Phrased as what a person can do. Status is what the code actually supports — read, traced, and in the core loop’s case unit-tested.

Get in

  • I can sign in with Apple so my places are stored in my own iCloud

    works

    But you are almost never shown this screen: iOS silently re-authenticates a cached Apple credential. On a simulator it cannot complete at all, and the error alert is destroyed before it can render.

  • I can sign in with Google

    broken

    No OAuth client ID is configured, so the button cannot work. It would buy nothing if it did — CloudKit is keyed to the device’s iCloud account, not to who signed in.

  • I can be warned when my device has no iCloud, so I know nothing will sync

    broken

    The check only guards the Google path, which nobody can complete. Apple sign-in skips it entirely.

Learn what this is

  • I can read what the app is for

    caveats

    Three pages. The pitch is duplicated across two of them, the Skip idea is explained abstractly rather than shown, and it promises sharing that does not exist.

  • I can practise by tapping a verdict on a sample place

    works

    The strongest part of onboarding. A real card, three real chips, and it will not let you past until you tap one.

  • I can skip onboarding and get on with it

    broken

    Skipping marks onboarding seen but never marks detection done — and nothing re-offers it. You are stranded on an empty map with no way to scan.

Find places

  • I can let SkyLine read my photo library and find everywhere I have been

    once per install

    The core premise, and it works: 25,936 photos clustered into episodes by GPS and time. But it runs exactly once per install. The re-entry point is written, documented, and has zero call sites.

  • I can understand what it is about to read before I grant access

    works

    A four-point primer runs before the system prompt: it reads date and location, your whole history, nothing leaves the device.

  • I can watch it work and stop it early

    works

    Live count of photos, trips and places, with “Stop and keep these” that keeps what it found.

  • I can be told honestly when it finds nothing

    works

    Six distinct endings — no photos, no locations, no trips, nothing substantial, access denied — each with its own copy and its own way forward. None framed as an error.

  • I can re-scan later when I have new photos

    broken

    There is no entry point anywhere in the app. Delete and reinstall is the only way.

Judge them

  • I can judge a place Worth it, Fine or Skip in one tap

    works

    Three 64pt stamps at thumb height, or swipe. The verdict writes immediately; nothing gates it. Well tested.

  • I can undo a verdict I got wrong

    works

    Four-second toast after every card, plus a persistent undo in the top bar.

  • I can say why, at the moment I remember why

    works

    “Add a note” on the undo toast, one tap away at the only moment you still remember.

  • I can skip a card and decide later

    works

    Deferrals write nothing and re-queue at the end of the deck.

  • I can see what I concluded when the deck runs out

    broken

    The summary is the only screen that states the product’s thesis in words. On the onboarding path it is torn down on the same frame it appears, because onboarding advances on deck finished rather than on the Done button. No first-time user has ever seen it.

  • I can come back to the places the scan found that I did not get to

    caveats

    The queue persists correctly and is unit-tested. But it reconciles on an identifier that the store’s own dedup silently drops, so cards you already judged replay and their trip never retires.

Look back

  • I can browse everywhere I have been, grouped by country

    broken

    Every photo-detected place has no country, so they all file under one “Unknown” heading, the summary card reads 0 countries, and no flags render.

  • I can search my places by name, city or country

    works

  • I can filter to just the ones I loved, or just the ones to skip

    works

    The filter chips double as the tally.

  • I can see the whole log as one bar of colour

    works

    And it doubles as the legend for the rails on every row.

  • I can open a place and see every time I went

    works

    Repeat visits get a trajectory card showing how my verdict changed between them.

  • I can read dates in the timezone of the place, not mine

    broken

    The timezone is never set on photo-detected places, so a 1am Tokyo photo shows in your own timezone.

  • I can write and edit a note on any visit

    works

  • I can get directions to a place

    works

See it on a globe

  • I can see my logged places on a rotating globe

    caveats

    Up to 16 name pills and coloured dots. Everything else on the globe is flight-tracker leftovers — arcs, airport codes, country tinting.

  • I can tap a place on the globe to open it

    broken

    The tap is received and printed to the console. Nothing else happens.

  • I can reach my log quickly

    broken

    The sheet the entire app lives in cannot appear until the globe reports ready — a three-second gate on a WebGL scene fetched from a CDN.

Trips, flights, boarding passes

  • I can create a trip by hand with dates and a destination

    works

    Vestigial: photo episodes already are trips, detected automatically.

  • I can find the places from one trip’s photos

    works

    But it requires you to hand-type the trip first — a leftover concept as a prerequisite for the core loop.

  • I can keep a day-by-day timeline of what I did

    caveats

    Attachments are dropped on save, editing an entry erases its coordinates and region, and Edit / Share / Delete on the trip menu are all no-ops.

  • I can scan a boarding pass with my camera

    broken

    There is no camera code in the app. The button opens a photo picker.

  • I can have a scanned pass read automatically

    broken

    The default path uploads the photo and your user id to a personal OpenRouter proxy for GPT-4o. The confirmation screen then refuses any pass more than 24 hours old, so past trips can never be logged.

  • I can see my flights

    caveats

    Four fabricated demo flights are seeded on first run and imported into your log as airports you have visited.

Account

  • I can switch between light and dark

    works

  • I can sign out

    works

  • I can change my display name and photo

    broken

    The name lives only in UserDefaults and is wiped by sign-out or the next Apple sign-in.

  • I can see my totals

    caveats

    Counts trips, countries and flights — three numbers that read 0 / 0 / 0 for a user who only ever scanned their photos.
